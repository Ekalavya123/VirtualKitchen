Fluffy Pancakes (serves 4)

Ingredients: 200 g plain flour, 2 tsp baking powder, 1 tbsp sugar, a pinch of salt, 2 eggs, 300 ml milk, 30 g melted butter, plus butter for the pan.

1. In a large bowl, whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt together.
2. In a jug, beat the eggs with the milk and the melted butter.
3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and whisk until just combined; a few lumps are fine. Rest the batter for 5 minutes.
4. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and add a little butter.
5. Pour about 60 ml of batter per pancake. Cook for 2 minutes until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook for 1 more minute until golden.
6. Keep warm and repeat with the remaining batter. Serve with maple syrup.
