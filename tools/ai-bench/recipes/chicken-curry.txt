Simple Chicken Curry (serves 4)

For the marinade: 600 g boneless chicken thighs cut into pieces, 150 g plain yogurt, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp salt.
For the curry: 3 tbsp oil, 2 onions finely sliced, 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 2 tomatoes chopped, 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp ground cumin, 200 ml water, fresh coriander.

1. Mix the chicken with the yogurt, turmeric, chilli powder and salt. Marinate for at least 30 minutes.
2. Heat the oil in a heavy pan over medium heat. Fry the onions for 10 minutes, stirring often, until deep golden.
3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add the tomatoes, garam masala and cumin and cook for 5 minutes until the tomatoes break down and the oil separates.
5. Add the marinated chicken and cook on high heat for 5 minutes, stirring.
6. Pour in the water, cover and simmer on low heat for 20 minutes until the chicken is cooked through. Check that the chicken is no longer pink inside; if it is, simmer 5 more minutes.
7. Garnish with chopped coriander and serve with rice.
