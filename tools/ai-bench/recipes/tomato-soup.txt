Roasted Tomato Soup (serves 4)

Ingredients: 1 kg ripe tomatoes, 1 onion, 4 garlic cloves, 2 tbsp olive oil, 500 ml vegetable stock, 1 tsp sugar, salt and pepper, a handful of basil, 50 ml cream.

1. Heat the oven to 200 C. Halve the tomatoes, quarter the onion and leave the garlic cloves whole.
2. Spread them on a tray, drizzle with olive oil, season with salt and pepper and roast for 30 minutes until soft and slightly charred.
3. Tip everything into a pot, add the stock and sugar and simmer for 10 minutes over low heat.
4. Add the basil and blend until smooth. If the soup is too thick, add a little water.
5. Taste and adjust the seasoning. Stir in the cream and serve hot.
