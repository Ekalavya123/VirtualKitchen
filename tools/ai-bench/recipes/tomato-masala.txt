Simple Tomato Masala (serves 2)

Ingredients: 2 onions, 2 tbsp oil, 3 ripe tomatoes (chopped), 1/2 tsp turmeric powder, salt.

1. Chop the onions finely on a cutting board.
2. Add the oil to a frying pan over medium heat.
3. Add the chopped onions to the pan.
4. Add the chopped tomatoes to the pan.
5. Add the turmeric and stir everything together.
6. Cook until the mixture softens into a thick masala.
